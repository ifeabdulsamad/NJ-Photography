About NJ

With over 10 years of experience, I specialize in landscape, portrait, and travel photography. My work is driven by a desire to tell stories through light and shadow, freezing moments that evoke emotion and wonder.

Whether I'm hiking through misty mountains or exploring bustling city streets, I'm always looking for that perfect shot.
